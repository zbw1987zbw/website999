Electric Precision Trimmer - Compact Detail Grooming

Professional electric precision trimmers designed for detailed grooming tasks. Featuring compact and ergonomic designs with precision blades, these trimmers are perfect for beard shaping, nose hair trimming, sideburns detailing, and edge trimming. Equipped with long-lasting lithium batteries, USB/USB-C charging, LED indicators, and waterproof ratings up to IPX6. Ideal for both personal use and professional grooming kits.
