Shaver - Electric Rechargeable Models with LED Display

Professional electric shavers with advanced lithium battery technology, IPX6/IPX7 waterproof rating, LED display, and multiple floating blade heads. Available in 3-head, 6-head, and reciprocating models. Each shaver is engineered for a smooth, close shave with intelligent battery management and travel-friendly features. Ideal for OEM/ODM customization with multiple optional accessories.
