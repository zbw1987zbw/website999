Hair Curler - Professional Ceramic Barrel Models

Professional hair curlers with ceramic barrels for long-lasting, beautiful curls. Our IBH series offers 3 models with different barrel sizes (19mm, 25mm, 32mm) for various curl styles from tight ringlets to loose waves. Features include adjustable temperature control, fast heat-up technology, ionic technology, cool tip for safe handling, and 360° swivel cords for easy maneuvering.
