About Ingboex

Who We Are

Company Profile

Wenzhou Ingboex Technology Co., Ltd. specializing in the production, processing, research, development, and sales of personal care appliances over 18 years.

Located in Ouhai District, China's razor production base "Wenzhou, China", our products cover electric grooming sets, electric hair clippers, electric shavers, electric foot sharpeners, electric hair puller sets and more.

Product diversification, serialization and innovation are the implementation standards of our company. We have established a comprehensive ISO9001 quality management system, and our products meet CE, GS, EMC, EC, and ROHS requirements.

Our products are exported to Europe, North America, South America, the Middle East and Southeast Asia, trusted by brands and distributors worldwide.

The factory is equipped with 5 production lines, more than 40 professional injection molding machines, large-scale ultrasonic equipment, automatic painting assembly line, salt spray tester, and other advanced manufacturing and testing facilities.

Our Mission

To provide global customers with innovative, high-quality personal care appliances through continuous technological advancement and strict quality control.

Our Vision

To become a world-leading OEM/ODM partner in the personal care appliance industry.

Our Values

Quality First, Customer First, Innovation, Integrity — these core values drive everything we do at INGBOEX.

Quality Standards

Certifications

International certifications that demonstrate our commitment to quality and safety

ISO9001

Certified quality management system ensuring consistent product quality and continuous improvement across all production processes.

CE

Conformité Européenne marking — our products comply with EU health, safety, and environmental protection standards.

GS

Geprüfte Sicherheit — German safety certification confirming our products meet rigorous safety requirements.

EMC

Electromagnetic Compatibility certification ensuring our devices operate without causing electromagnetic interference.

ROHS

Restriction of Hazardous Substances compliance — all materials used are environmentally safe and free from restricted substances.

LVD

Low Voltage Directive (2014/35/EU) compliance — our electrical appliances meet EU safety requirements for equipment operating within specified voltage limits.
